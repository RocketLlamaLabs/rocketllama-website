Audit a whole marketing system from one URL.

Enter a website, social handles, or both. The audit reads the site, runs Google PageSpeed, finds the Google profile and competitors, asks an AI search engine who it recommends and checks every social profile. Each audit gets a link you can send to the prospect.

What it covers

  • Website and conversion

    Click-to-call, booking, forms, chat, proof, accessibility.

  • Search engines

    Titles, headings, content, sitemap, indexing, share previews.

  • AI answer engines

    A live AI search for the trade and city, schema, llms.txt, AI crawler access.

  • Speed

    Google PageSpeed on a phone and real-visitor Core Web Vitals.

  • Google profile

    Rating, reviews, hours and photos against the top local competitors.

  • Social media

    Every profile found, its size and how it is run.

  • Ads and tracking

    Pixels, conversion tags, call tracking and the public ad libraries.

  • Lead handling

    Speed to lead, phone answering, CRM and follow-up.
