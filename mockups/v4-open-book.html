    Questions

    The things every owner asks.

    What do you report every month?

    Booked jobs by source, closed-job value by source, cost per booked job, return per dollar spent, median reply time, and what we changed. Not clicks, impressions or lead counts.

    What does it cost?

    About 10% of revenue in three lines a month: a $797 platform base, our work itemised, and ad spend billed to you directly by Google and Meta. A $500K company runs about $4,250 a month. Setup from scratch is $5,000 to $7,500, refunded if you do not like the website design we show you first.

    What happens if I leave?

    You leave with everything: the website code transferred to your GitHub, the CRM data exported, the phone numbers ported, and the ad accounts, which were always yours. We help the next provider get set up for 30 days.

    Is there a contract?

    A one-page agreement, month to month, cancel with 30 days notice. The whole agreement is on the pricing page.

    What if a month is bad?

    You see it in the report, with the reason and what we are changing. The floor is two dollars back for every dollar spent over a rolling quarter. If we miss it, we work free until we are back above it.

    Does the AI replace my office staff?

    No. The phone rings your team first. The AI picks up only when nobody can, takes the details, and books a callback or an appointment instead of sending the caller to voicemail.
