What works, kept

  1. 1Numbers with an owner’s name. Case cards show the result, the owner, the trade, the city and the dates. Every version uses that format.
  2. 2Booked jobs, not clicks. Scorpion and Valve+Meter own this line. We make it concrete with the CRM window and the return calculator.
  3. 3AI search in the hero. Scorpion and Townsquare say it. Version 2 shows it: three AI engines asked for a plumber, and whether you are the answer.
  4. 4You own everything. Blue Corona’s best line. It is in the proof strip on every version, and Version 4 has a switch that proves it.
  5. 5Free competitor analysis. RYNO, Blue Corona and 1SEO gate it. Version 2 runs it in the hero with no email.
  6. 6Click-to-call in the header. Half of them have it. We add the sticky mobile bar.

What fails, and the opening

  1. 1Eight of ten hide the price. Every version puts the three lines on a pricing page. Version 4 goes further and shows the whole invoice and the agreement.
  2. 2Twelve-month contracts. Month to month is in the proof strip and the comparison table.
  3. 3Zero city pages, national sprawl. Version 3 makes the city the unit of the whole site. All versions carry a Phoenix address and city-level schema.
  4. 4Seven to eleven form fields. Four fields, then optional questions after booking.
  5. 5Jargon. No AEO, GEO, LSA, CSR or GBP anywhere. Google profile, search and AI search, the phone.
  6. 6Sloppy claims and schema. Blank addresses, typos, placeholders, stale stats. Every number on our pages names its source and the schema matches the footer.
  7. 7Nothing to try. Only CI Web Group ships tools. Versions 2, 3 and 4 each lead with one: the audit, the map, the open report and the live demo.

Ranking lists consulted for the shortlist: Housecall Pro, Harris and Ward, Zio Advertising, Revved Digital, all 2026. Vendor-written, used only to pick the ten sites.

Search and AI optimization

Five search engines, three AI engines, one set of work

Most of it is the same hygiene the agencies above skip. The pieces that differ by engine are below. Everything marked "in the mockup" is already in the four sites.

Google

own index · feeds Gemini and AI Overviews

  • Organization, ProfessionalService, FAQPage, HowTo and speakable schema in the mockup
  • Google Business Profile complete, posted weekly, every review answered
  • Core Web Vitals: real text, next/image, fonts with swap
  • A page per trade per city

Bing

own index · feeds ChatGPT browsing and Copilot

  • Bing Webmaster Tools verified, sitemap submitted
  • IndexNow ping on every publish
  • Bing Places listing matching the Google profile
  • Same schema as Google in the mockup

Yahoo

results from Bing

  • Covered by the Bing work above
  • Yahoo Local pulls from Yelp: keep the Yelp listing complete and matched

DuckDuckGo

results from Bing · maps from Apple

  • Covered by the Bing work above
  • Apple Business Connect listing with the same name, address, phone and hours
  • Yelp reviews surface in DuckDuckGo local results

Brave

own index · feeds Brave Leo and some AI tools

  • Rewards original text over thin or duplicated pages
  • No special markup: the city pages with real, specific content are the work
  • Allow its crawler in robots.txt

ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity

Bing · Google · own crawler plus Bing

  • Sourced statistics in full sentences in the mockup
  • FAQ answers that match the schema word for word in the mockup
  • Named author with Person schema in the mockup
  • Reddit, Yelp and Google reviews feed Perplexity: a reviews program is AI search
  • Allow GPTBot, Google-Extended, PerplexityBot and ClaudeBot. llms.txt is cheap hygiene, not a lever

Brand rules

Eight rules from brand book v1.0, held in every mockup

  1. 1Supplied logo files only, never redrawn or recolored. The footer wordmark sits on a white plate instead of being inverted.
  2. 2Clear space around every logo of at least the cap height of the R.
  3. 3Navy Montserrat Bold headings. 16px Inter body minimum. Nothing under 12px.
  4. 4One blue primary button per screen. The header and the mobile action bar are outlined navy, so the hero button is the one blue.
  5. 5Four colors only. The competitors’ greens, teals and yellows are gone. Photography sits under a flat navy overlay.
  6. 6No gradients. Every band, bar, ring and chart is a flat fill.
  7. 7Sentence case everywhere. No exclamation marks. No all-caps headings, no emoji.
  8. 8One H1 per page, alt text on every logo, the mascot as favicon.
