Search and AI

Built in, not bolted on

✓
One entity, everywhere the sameOrganization and ProfessionalService schema with name, Phoenix address, phone, hours, service area, founder and services. The same details sit in the footer in real HTML. Competitors we read had blank addresses and a misspelled telephone key.
✓
FAQPage schema that matches the visible FAQSix questions, word for word on the pricing page. This is what Google and Gemini quote and what Perplexity cites.
✓
Speakable and plain answersThe H1 and lead are marked speakable. Every price and term is stated in a full sentence an engine can lift.
✓
Bing, Yahoo and DuckDuckGo in one moveYahoo and DuckDuckGo run on Bing’s index. Ship IndexNow pings on every publish, verify Bing Webmaster Tools, claim Bing Places, and keep Apple Business Connect and Yelp matched, which DuckDuckGo’s maps draw from.
✓
Brave and the AI enginesBrave keeps its own index and rewards original pages with real text. ChatGPT browses through Bing, Gemini through Google, Perplexity through its own crawler plus Bing. Allow their crawlers in robots.txt and keep the content in HTML, not images.
✓
Per-trade and per-city pages nextNone of the ten agencies has city pages. Phoenix, Scottsdale, Mesa, Chandler, Gilbert, Glendale, Tempe and Peoria, each with a trade and local reviews, is the biggest gap to take.
✓
Sourced numbers, named authorsEvery statistic names its source. Articles carry Brian’s byline with Person schema.
✓
The audit is HTML, not an appEvery check and result is real text, so an engine can read what the audit measures, and so a homeowner-side owner asking "what should my Google profile have" can be answered from it.
