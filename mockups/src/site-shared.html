What if I already get plenty of work from referrals?

Good. Referrals check you on Google before they call, and referrals do not scale when you want a third truck. We make sure what they find closes the deal, and we build the second engine for when you want it.

I already have a website and ad accounts. Can you use them?

Yes. We audit what you have on the first call, keep what is working and fix what is not. A site or ad account that is already set up simply lowers your setup fee, because there is less to build. We may tune them (tracking, landing pages, review schema, the way the phone is answered), but we never start over for the sake of it.

What if I want to leave?

You leave with everything: the website code, the data, the phone numbers, the ad accounts. We will help the next provider get set up. Month to month, cancel any time.

Can I build a website with AI for free?

You can, and it will look fine. Most AI-built sites are invisible to Google and to ChatGPT because nothing is structured underneath. Ours ship with the schema, the stars and the funnels wired in, on the same stack the professionals use, and you own the code.

Do you work outside Phoenix?

Yes. Everything is remote-capable, and we expand city by city. Phoenix owners get Brian in person for the proposal.
