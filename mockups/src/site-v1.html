Questions

The things every owner asks.

What if I already get plenty of work from referrals?

Good. Referrals check you on Google before they call, and referrals do not scale when you want a third truck. We make sure what they find closes the deal, and we build the second engine for when you want it.

I already have a website and ad accounts. Can you use them?

Yes. We audit what you have on the first call, keep what is working and fix what is not. A site or ad account that is already set up simply lowers your setup fee, because there is less to build. We may tune them (tracking, landing pages, review schema, the way the phone is answered), but we never start over for the sake of it.

What if I want to leave?

You leave with everything: the website code, the data, the phone numbers, the ad accounts. We will help the next provider get set up. Month to month, cancel any time.

Can I build a website with AI for free?

You can, and it will look fine. Most AI-built sites are invisible to Google and to ChatGPT because nothing is structured underneath. Ours ship with the schema, the stars and the funnels wired in, on the same stack the professionals use, and you own the code.

Quick rules

Eight rules, and where each one lives

  1. 1
    Use the supplied logo files. Never redraw, re-type, stretch, rotate or recolor a mark.

    Every logo in this file is the SVG master from /public/brand, embedded unchanged. In the navy footer the wordmark sits on a white plate instead of being inverted.

  2. 2
    Clear space around any logo equals the cap height of the R in the wordmark, on all sides.

    Header, footer plate and the About lockup all keep at least that much padding.

  3. 3
    Headings are navy in Montserrat Bold. Body is 16px Inter minimum. Nothing under 12px.

    Body is 16px, leads 18 to 20px, eyebrows 14px, mono sources 13px. The diagram’s smallest label is 12.5px at full width.

  4. 4
    One brand-blue primary button per screen. Secondary actions are outlined navy.

    The header button is outlined. Each page has exactly one blue button: the hero on Home, the closing band on Services, Industries, Pricing and About, and the submit on Book. The quiz result, the free scan and every trade chip are outlined.

  5. 5
    Four colors only: navy, blue, mist, white. The logo’s lighter blues and flame gradient stay inside the artwork.

    All UI color comes from the four staples and the derived tokens. The score ring, the diagram and the trade icons are blue and navy on mist. Photography from the holding site sits under a flat navy overlay.

  6. 6
    No gradients. Buttons, bands, charts and progress bars are flat navy, blue or mist.

    Every band, card, chip, the quiz progress bar and the 65 / 25 / 10 bar above is a flat fill. Shadows are navy at low opacity.

  7. 7
    Sentence case everywhere, including buttons and nav. No exclamation marks in UI.

    Check any heading, button, eyebrow, table header or nav item. Nothing is set in all caps, including the diagram labels that were capitals on the holding site.

  8. 8
    Every page has one H1 in real HTML text, alt text on every logo, and the favicon set.

    Each tab is its own page with a single H1. Logos carry alt text. The favicon is the mascot.

Voice check: capable, direct, light. The holding-site copy already leads with the outcome and says what it costs and how long it takes, so it is used as written. Placeholders remain for Branson’s and Madison’s bios and the video file. The scaled pricing illustration is marked as an illustration and defers to the walkthrough.
