Questions

Things people ask on the first call

How do you fix the price before you know the details?

The scope week exists for exactly that. We look at the real data, the real tools and the real edge cases, then write a one-page plan with the price and the date. If the plan changes later, the price changes in writing before any work happens.

Which AI models do you use?

Whichever fits the job and your data rules. Most agents run on the latest Claude models through your own API account, so the keys and the bills are yours.

Do you work with teams outside Arizona?

Yes. We are in Phoenix and most of our clients are not. Scope calls happen on video, and we will fly out for the Monday of a rescue if the slow thing is physical.

What happens after launch?

Every engagement includes support. After that you can keep us on a sprint, or hand the repo to your own team with the docs we wrote. Either is fine.

What we do

Quick rules

Eight rules, and where each one lives

  1. 1
    Use the supplied logo files. Never redraw, re-type, stretch, rotate or recolor a mark.

    Every logo in this file is the SVG master from /public/brand, embedded unchanged. In the navy footer the wordmark sits on a white plate instead of being inverted.

  2. 2
    Clear space around any logo equals the cap height of the R in the wordmark, on all sides.

    Header, footer plate and the About lockup all keep at least that much padding.

  3. 3
    Headings are navy in Montserrat Bold. Body is 16px Inter minimum. Nothing under 12px.

    Body is 16px, leads 18 to 20px, eyebrows and mono labels 13 to 14px.

  4. 4
    One brand-blue primary button per screen. Secondary actions are outlined navy.

    The header button is outlined. Each page has exactly one blue button: the hero on Home, the closing band on Services, Work and About, and the submit on Contact.

  5. 5
    Four colors only: navy, blue, mist, white. The logo’s lighter blues and flame gradient stay inside the artwork.

    All UI color comes from the four staples and the derived tokens. The mascot keeps its own palette.

  6. 6
    No gradients. Buttons, bands, charts and progress bars are flat navy, blue or mist.

    Every band, card, chip and the 65 / 25 / 10 bar above is a flat fill. Shadows are navy at low opacity.

  7. 7
    Sentence case everywhere, including buttons and nav. No exclamation marks in UI.

    Check any heading, button, eyebrow, table header or nav item. Nothing is set in all caps.

  8. 8
    Every page has one H1 in real HTML text, alt text on every logo, and the favicon set.

    Each tab is its own page with a single H1. Logos carry alt text. The favicon is the mascot.

Voice check: capable, direct, light. Lead with the outcome. Say what it costs and how long it takes. The pricing and the sample case studies are placeholders, labelled as such, until real numbers replace them.
