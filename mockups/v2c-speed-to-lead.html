Questions

The things every owner asks.

Why does 60 seconds matter?

Answer inside two minutes and 62% of leads convert. The average company answers in 42 minutes and converts 28%. Only 12% of contractors respond inside five minutes. Sources: ServiceTitan 2025, Hatch 2026.

What does it cost?

About 10% of revenue in three lines a month: a $797 platform base, our work itemised, and ad spend billed to you directly by Google and Meta. A $500K company runs about $4,250 a month. Setup from scratch is $5,000 to $7,500, refunded if you do not like the website design we show you first.

Does the AI replace my office staff?

No. The phone rings your team first. The AI picks up only when nobody can, takes the details, and books a callback or an appointment instead of sending the caller to voicemail.

Is there a contract?

No. Month to month, cancel any time. You leave with the website code, the data, the phone numbers and the ad accounts, and we help the next provider get set up.

Do you work outside Phoenix?

Yes. Everything is remote-capable, and we expand city by city. Phoenix owners get Brian in person for the proposal.
